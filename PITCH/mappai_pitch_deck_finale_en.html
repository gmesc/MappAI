02 · The Solution

Intelligence becomes visual

Real MappAI output — interactive Knowledge Graph generated automatically

D3.js engine: text → interactive semantic graphs. AI reflects the cognitive structure of the subject — it doesn't replace it.

Mind Map: hierarchical

L0 → L5, multi-phase, atomic branches. Ideal for indexes and visual hierarchies (DSA/ADHD).

Knowledge Graph: networked

Entities + 55+ relation types (causes, precedes, opposes, regulates...). Ideal for history, science, critical thinking.

Built-in accessibility

Focus cards, OpenDyslexic font, syllable breaks, text-to-speech, verbatim citations from sources (zero hallucinations), auto-generated quizzes and flashcards.

Validated results: Mind Map 77 nodes / 0 truncations · KG 38 nodes / 47% cross-links · 45 sec from a 20-page PDF.

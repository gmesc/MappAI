Product · Output Materials
Study materials — on screen and on paper.
Every map auto-generates ready-to-use study supports, digital or printable. The node labels in particular let students rebuild maps on paper and power recall and metacognition activities — a feature shaped by early feedback from pedagogists who tested the beta.
Branch synthesis — full prose of a branch, with citations back to the source.
Node dossier — a print-ready hierarchical outline of the whole map.
Printable node labels — cut out to build paper maps and run recall games.
Full Knowledge Graph — a real, dense map generated from a single text.
